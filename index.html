How it works

A week of Sunday School, start to finish.

Choose your role to see how Gospelar fits into your week.

01 Monday to Saturday

Read the daily devotional

Seven readings a week tied to Sunday’s lesson — a prayer, a reflection and something to apply.

02 Sunday

Study the lesson in class

Open this week’s lesson in your own language, look up every Bible reference and sing the suggested hymns.

03 After class

Take the quiz

Answer a short quiz, earn points and see where you stand on your class leaderboard.

01 Before Sunday

Prepare from the manual

The full lesson, teacher notes and suggested answers to every question are in the app.

02 In class

Mark attendance in seconds

Tap who’s present and award marks for memory verses and answers — no network needed.

03 Back online

It syncs by itself

Everything saved on the phone uploads automatically, so your church sees the numbers.

01 Set up

Invite your teachers

Approve teachers who join with your church’s invite code and organise classes and branches.

02 Every Sunday

Follow engagement live

See attendance, lesson completion and top learners across your congregation as they happen.

03 All year

Run your events

Open registration for retreats, camps and conventions — for individuals, families and church groups.
