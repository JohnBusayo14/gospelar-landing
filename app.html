What’s inside

One app for the whole of Sunday School.

Everything a student, teacher or church leader needs each week — in one place.

Weekly lessons

The full quarter for Children, Intermediate, Youth and Adult — background, lesson parts, memory verse and questions.

Daily devotionals

Seven readings a week tied to Sunday’s lesson, each with a prayer, a reflection and something to apply.

Two hymn books

The Gospel Hymn Book and the Church Hymnal, searchable by number or first line, in your language.

The Holy Bible, offline

King James Version built in; ASV, WEB, BBE and the Yorùbá, Igbo and Hausa Bibles download once.

Quizzes and leaderboards

A short quiz after every lesson, with points and a friendly class leaderboard.

Sunday School videos

Teaching videos for every age group, ready to watch in the app.

Get started

Up and running in three steps.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? WhatsApp us or email askgospelars@gmail.com.

Is Gospelar free?
Yes — Gospelar is free for students. Download it from Google Play and sign up with your email address.
Does it work without internet?
Yes. Lessons, hymns and the King James Bible work offline once they’ve been opened, and teachers can mark attendance with no network. Everything syncs automatically when you’re back online.
Which languages are available?
English, Yorùbá, Igbo and Hausa. Change the language in Settings and every screen, lesson and notification follows.
Is there an iPhone version?
Not yet — Gospelar is on Android today and the iPhone version is coming soon. Follow us on Instagram or X to hear when it launches.
I’m a teacher. How do I join my church?
When you create your account, choose Teacher and enter the invite code your church admin gave you. Your church admin approves your account, and then you can sign in and create your classes.
I forgot my password. What do I do?
Contact your church admin or Gospelar support. They can reset it and give you a temporary password — then change it in the app under Settings → Change Password.
What happens to my data?
We never show ads or sell your data. Your scores are only visible to your teacher and church admin. Read the full Privacy Policy.
